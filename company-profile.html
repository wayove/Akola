Home / Company Profile
Est. 1981 · Akola, Maharashtra

Grown from a single facility into a Nutrition Specialist.

With our strong emphasis on using the ideal raw materials and employing innovative technologies over the years, Akola Chemicals has emerged as one of the most promising business entities in our domain.

Known as a leading manufacturer of Hydrolysed Proteins, Manganese Amino Acid Chelate, Calcium Caseinate and mineral chelates, we have directed our endeavours to meet the desired needs and requirements of our clients in an efficient as well as precise manner.

Since our establishment, we have grown into a Nutrition Specialist producing hydrolysed proteins for the Agricultural, Animal Feed, Food, Nutraceutical and Cosmetics Industries.

Founded
1981

Akola, Maharashtra — over four decades of continuous manufacturing and R&D.

Manufacturing

Two divisions, each purpose-built.

Agro Division

Manufactures plant and animal nutrition products from varied protein sources. Licensed to manufacture Micronutrient Fertilizers.

Speciality Chemicals Division

Manufactures premium-grade hydrolyzed proteins for Food, Nutraceutical and Cosmetic industries. FDA-approved facilities with FSSAI and cosmetic manufacturing licenses.
